Where Colonial Charm Meets Royal Indian Flavours

Established in 1940 during the British colonial era, Kwality began as a sophisticated tearoom and dining lounge within the iconic colonnades of Connaught Place.

Over the decades, it became a beloved New Delhi gathering place. Today, its vintage character, rich dark wood and classic dishes continue the heritage dining experience.

Meticulously presented with a timeless colonial aesthetic, Kwality remains a destination for celebratory meals and generations of diners.
