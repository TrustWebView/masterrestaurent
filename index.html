Where Colonial Charm Meets Royal Indian Flavours

Established in 1940 during the British colonial era, Kwality began as a sophisticated tearoom and dining lounge within the iconic colonnades of Connaught Place.

Over the decades, it became the intellectual heartbeat of New Delhi. Celebrated painters like M.F. Husain, freedom fighters, prime ministers, and international diplomats gathered under its crystal chandeliers to debate, celebrate, and feast.

Today, meticulously restored with vintage archival photographs, bevelled mirrors, and rich dark wood, Kwality remains a timeless beacon of culinary excellence.
