Our Living History

Where Generations Gathered & History Was Made

Established during the British Raj in 1940 at the iconic Regal Building in Connaught Place, Kwality began as a cozy tearoom and ice cream parlour for American GIs and British officers before evolving into the gastronomic heart of New Delhi.

For more than eight decades, Kwality has hosted Prime Ministers, Bollywood legends like Raj Kapoor and Dev Anand, foreign ambassadors, and discerning families who consider dining here a cherished family tradition.

Delhi's #1 Cult Dish

The Legend of Kwality's Original Chana Bhatura

Decades before modern food trends, Kwality perfected what is universally acknowledged as India’s most iconic Chana Bhatura. A dish so celebrated that generations of Delhiites trace their fondest memories to this single culinary masterpiece.

  • The Giant Cloud Bhatura: Feather-light, crisp exterior puffed to perfection without excessive oil.
  • Dark Pindi Chole: Slow-simmered for 18 hours with dried anardana, amla, and our 1940 secret spice potli.
  • Heritage Accompaniments: Served with homemade carrot-chilli pickle, raw onions, and spiced mint chutney.
